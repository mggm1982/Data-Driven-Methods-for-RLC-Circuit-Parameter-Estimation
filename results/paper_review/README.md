# Descriptive reviewer summaries (generated)

Generated by `python3 paper/generate_tables.py`; freshness checked with `--check`.
Only archived measured JSON summaries and training histories are read. Original
measurements are not modified; no training, fitting, raw prediction inspection,
new held-out tests, significance tests, or model selection is performed.

## Tables and machine-readable data

- `paper/tables/noise.tex`: full 50 ms / 5001-point grid, noise 0/1/2/5/10%,
  CMLP/NMLP/NLS, R/L/C median relative errors. One row per noise/method.
- `paper/tables/seed_spread.tex`: all six windows and four densities, 10% noise,
  CMLP/NMLP R/L/C medians. One row per observation/method.
- `summaries.json`: all available relative-error statistics (mean, median, p95,
  max) and nonempty regimes for every grid/level/method; all archived sampling
  threshold rates, including tails and clean conditions; per-seed means and
  realization counts; distinct-checkpoint training epoch inventory.

Neural table cells are mean +/- descriptive sample SD (n=3 training seeds,
20261014/15/16, denominator n-1). First average each condition statistic over
three noise realizations *within each seed*, then average those three seed
means and compute their sample SD. At zero noise use the single clean condition
once per seed, not three copies. These are averages of statistics, not pooled
circuit medians/quantiles/maxima. Repeated realizations share circuits. SD is
not a confidence interval, standard error, or circuit/noise-realization spread.
NLS averages realizations only and has null seed SD, not zero seed SD.

Relative errors are converted from fractions to percent before both mean and
SD. Rates are already percentages and are not multiplied by 100; SD is in
percentage points. Original compact windows/sampling/sparse_tails/error_rates
fragments are unchanged for width; their complete descriptive spreads are in
`summaries.json`, with window/density medians additionally in `seed_spread.tex`.
The new fragments are caption-free tabulars included by the manuscript.
Headers R/L/C denote resistance/inductance/capacitance. Generation does not edit
`main.tex`; TeX layout still needs visual verification.

Sources: `results/noise/results.json`, `results/fitting/results.json`,
`results/windows/results.json`, `results/sampling/results.json`, and
`results/sampling/error_rates.json`. Historical `original` is excluded from
matched three-seed summaries and epoch inventory (no full history archived).
